Indication — why the case happened

A CPT code cannot tell a trauma ex-lap from an elective one. One tap here is what turns “67 abdominal cases” into “41 trauma laparotomies”. No diagnosis, no identifiers.

Case weight (acuity)

Applies to OR cases only; other activity is counted, never weighted. This is a billing-derived case weight (CMS base units + ASA + qualifying circumstances) — consistent across the force, not a measure of how hard the case was.

Autonomy — per phase

0 attending directs/performs · 1 attending present, I execute · 2 attending available, I decide · 3 I am alone. Score the level that held for most of the phase — not the worst moment, not the best. “No crisis occurred” is NA, never 0.

This separates policy from need: a level 1 because the site requires it is a different fact from a level 1 because the case needed two sets of hands.

Backfilling from the chart? Leave autonomy blank rather than guess — a blank is honest data; a guessed score poisons the mean.

A day with no cases is data only if the reason is captured. The first two are clinical days (you were at the bedside, the cases were not there). The rest are Army time away from the bedside — the denominator the force cannot see today.
